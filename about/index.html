Our story

It started at an HR desk, not a whiteboard.

Most HR software is designed by people who have never run a payroll or chased a signed contract, and it shows. HR teams end up working around their own system: exporting to spreadsheets, keying the same employee details into three places, waiting weeks for a report that should take a minute.

JaliHR began in Australia as the answer to that. We knew where the real HR work happens because we had been doing it, so we designed every screen around those moments. One employee record. Every module reading from it. Analytics that are ready the moment your data is in.

It was built to travel from the start. Payroll, leave and labour law change at every border, so JaliHR handles them the way each country actually works. Today HR teams far beyond Australia open it every morning.

Pricing

What it costs. What you get.

Project-based. No retainers. No surprises.

How we price

JaliHR prices per project, not per hour or per month. You know the scope and the fee before we start. Every engagement begins with a discovery call at no charge.

We do not do open-ended retainers. If the scope changes, we agree a new price before the work changes.

How we deliver

  1. 1 Discovery. We understand your situation. One call, sometimes two.
  2. 2 Diagnosis. We identify the root cause, not just the symptom.
  3. 3 Recommendation. We tell you what we think you should do.
  4. 4 Delivery. We produce the artefact: a report, a framework, a scoping deck, or a vendor shortlist.
  5. 5 Handoff. You own everything we produce. No lock-in.

Most projects close in 2 to 6 weeks depending on complexity.

What is not included

We do not implement software on your behalf. We do not manage vendors after handoff. We do not provide legal advice.

If you need ongoing people operations support, we can discuss what that looks like separately.

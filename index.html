HR intelligence

People analytics, without the people analyst.

An HR system should hand you the intelligence, not hand you one more spreadsheet to build it from. JaliHR is built on a foundation where analytics drive decisions instead of reporting numbers. Decisions like who to offer, who to promote, where to hire, how much to pay and what to train can be derived automatically from the moment your data is in.

This means HR people do not need to become Excel formula gurus, unless they want to, or beg the IT guy, or girl, for a leadership report for six weeks that never comes. Attend that meeting, open analytics, present.

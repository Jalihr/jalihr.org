01

HR Technology

We fix what broke. We make sure what you acquire works from day one.

Most HRIS implementations fail before they go live. Wrong platform, wrong scope, no change management. We come in before the mess or after it.

We acquire: We evaluate platforms against your actual headcount, payroll complexity, and budget. We run the demo, scope the contract, and hand you a recommendation you can act on.

We rescue: Already live on a system that is not working? We diagnose why, define what recovery looks like, and run it.

What this covers

  • HRIS selection and vendor evaluation
  • Implementation scoping and oversight
  • Post-go-live rescue and stabilisation
  • Platform comparison reports (Factorial, PaySpace, Deel, and others)

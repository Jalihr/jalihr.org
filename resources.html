Recommended

What we actually tell clients.

These are the platforms we recommend most often, per category. Not sponsored. Not affiliated.

All-in-one HRIS

Factorial

Clean UI, reasonable price, and improving Africa coverage. Good starting point for most SMEs.

Payroll — East Africa

PaySpace

Compliance depth across Kenya, Uganda, and Rwanda. Our go-to for larger, compliance-sensitive employers.

Global contractors

Deel

Best-in-class for distributed teams and EOR. If you have contractors or cross-border staff, this is the benchmark.

Compensation data

Pave

Strongest global data, but Africa coverage is limited — Uganda data not yet released, Kenya moderate. We flag this to every client.
